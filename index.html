Sourcing Process

From Spec to Shipment in Three Steps

Our streamlined B2B sourcing workflow ensures quality-verified fabric reaches your factory floor on schedule.

01

Spec Submission & Sample Request

Select from our fabric catalog or send a reference sample. Share your required GSM, composition, width, and color specifications through WhatsApp or our inquiry form.

02

Lab Dip & Counter-Sample Approval

We produce lab dip samples for exact color swatch matching and GSM validation. Counter-samples are dispatched for your approval before bulk production begins.

03

Bulk Circular Knitting & Export Dispatch

Your approved specifications enter our circular knitting production line. Quality inspection, finishing, and door-to-port shipment logistics are managed end-to-end.
